Mathematical Notes

Here are several notes I wrote while attending various courses, together with further explorations in Mathematics. Each entry includes the last modified date, download links and a description of the content.

Most of these notes are in Italian; Modular Forms is in English. An asterisk (*) marks material that is partly or entirely handwritten. I intend to revise and expand the notes in English, and possibly consolidate some of them.

Modular Forms

Updated 11/08/2026
English

PDF
These notes were originally based on the course taught by Professors Andrea Maffei and Davide Lombardo in the academic year 2020/2021 at the University of Pisa. The present version contains substantial additional material related to the main topics, which I have included according to my own interests.

This GitHub repository also contains the Julia/OSCAR code supporting the algorithmic computations associated with the theoretical objects introduced in the notes. Each .jl file includes documentation for the implemented function together with several examples of their use.

Main topics of the notes:
Topics - Möbius transformations, hyperbolic geometry, Fuchsian groups and Riemann surfaces. - Classical modular forms for SL2(ℤ): q-expansions, Eisenstein series, cusp forms, the modular discriminant, the j-invariant, and the graded algebra of modular forms.
- Number-theoretic aspects of modular forms: Poisson summation, Bernoulli numbers, theta functions and lattices, infinite products, the Ramanujan-Serre operators, and applications to sum of squares.
- Congruence subgroups and modular curves: cusp points, compactification, genus formulas, modular forms of higher level, the q-expansion principle, Sturm's bound, and the Petersson product.
- Representation theory and Hecke theory: group representations, Haar measures, Hecke algebras, Hecke and diamond operators, and the theory of old and new cusp forms.
- L-functions associated with modular forms: Mellin transforms, Dirichlet series, Euler products, Dirichlet characters and functional equations.
- Complex tori and elliptic curves: elliptic functions, periods, torsion points, and the passage between lattices and projective cubic curves. - Moduli interpretations of modular curves and Hecke operators, modular polynomials, Hauptmoduls and explicit equations for 𝕏0(n)

A note on dihedral groups

Brief overview of dihedral groups and their main properties. For each n ∈ ℕ, with n ≥ 3, the n-th dihedral group is the group of plane transformations mapping a regular n-gon onto itself.

Lie Algebras and Lie Groups

Updated 16/06/2021
Italian

PDF
My notes from the course taught by professors Giovanni Gaiffi and Enrico Le Donne in the academic year 2018/2019.
Main topics of the course:
Topics - Lie Algebras: definitions, homomorphisms and first properties. Killing form.
- Representation theory of Lie algebras. Casimir element. Weyl Theorem. The algebra sl(2,K) and its irreducible representations. Standard cyclic modules. Weight lattice and examples.
- Root systems: Dynkin diagrams and their complete classification. Weyl groups. Connection between Lie Algebras and root system: Lie theorem and semisimplicity criterion. Toral algebras.
- Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem and Serre relations.
- Lie Groups: definitions, homomorphisms and first properties. The Lie Algebra of GL(n,ℝ). One-parameter subgroups and subalgebras. Exponential map. Adjoint representation. Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff formula.

Elements of Complex Analysis (*)

Updated 21/02/2022
Italian

PDF
My notes from the course taught by professor Marco Abate in the academic year 2020/2021.
Main topics of the course:
Topics - Complements of complex analysis in one variable: compact-open topology and topology of uniform convergence on compact sets. Convergence of sequences of holomorphic functions; compactness in the space of holomorphic functions.
- Hurwitz theorems. Schwarz lemma. Automorphisms of the unit disk, of the half-plane, of the complex plane, of the Riemann sphere.
- Poincaré distance. Wolff-Denjoy theorem. Riemann uniformization theorem. Approximation of holomorphic functions; applications. Construction of global functions starting from local data.
- Introduction to complex analysis in several variables: definitions and examples. Cauchy-Riemann conditions and consequences. Analytic continuation principle. Cauchy integral formula. Cauchy inequalities. Maximum principle. Weierstrass, Montel and Vitali theorems. Hartogs extension theorem. Domains of holomorphy. Convex and pseudoconvex domains. Plurisubharmonic functions. Levi problem and its solution.

Algebraic Geometry C

Updated 28/07/2023
Italian

PDF
A list of definitions and statements from the course taught by professor Marco Franciosi in the academic year 2022/2023.
Main topics of the course:
Topics - Definition and examples of Riemann surfaces. Holomorphic and meromorphic functions on Riemann surfaces. Group actions and finite Riemann surfaces; Hurwitz bound for the number of automorphisms of a curve of genus greater than 1.
- Differential forms and integration on a Riemann surface.
- Divisors on compact Riemann surfaces, principal divisors, linear equivalence, canonical divisors. Linear systems and maps to projective spaces. The Riemann-Roch theorem. Serre duality with applications: embedding compact Riemann surfaces in projective space, curves of low genus.
- Projective curves: Castelnuovo's bound, general position lemma, flexes, Weierstrass points. Definition of the Jacobian variety, Abel-Jacobi map. The theorem of Abel and Jacobi.

The file includes notes on some theoretical parts concerning sheaves, Čech cohomology, vector bundles, divisors and base points
