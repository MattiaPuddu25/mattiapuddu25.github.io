  1. On the geometric degree of the prolongation varieties of the differential system \(x_1' = x_2^2\), \(x_2' = x_1^2\) (2026). Available at: arXiv:2611.06638.

    Abstract

    A prolongation variety of a polynomial differential system \(S\) is an algebraic variety defined by the equations of \(S\) and their derivatives up to a given order. We compute the geometric degree of the prolongation varieties associated with the system \[ \begin{cases} x_1' = x_2^2\\ x_2' = x_1^2 \end{cases} \] in terms of the prolongation order, thereby proving a conjecture stated by Gleb Pogudin in Section 2, Question 6 of “Some Open Problems in Differential Algebra”.

    Our approach is motivated from the beginning by doing computational experiments in OSCAR in order to find the number of solutions of a zero-dimensional system using algebraic Thomas decomposition. The result will follow from a coprimality condition on a family of polynomials with integer coefficients, which is verified by showing that their roots satisfy a strong interlacing property. Finally, we propose an extension of the conjecture for the systems \[ \begin{cases} x_1' = x_2^n\\ x_2' = x_1^2. \end{cases} \] with \(n > 2\).
